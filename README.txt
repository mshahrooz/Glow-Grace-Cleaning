GLOW GRACE CLEANING — STATIC MULTI-PAGE WEBSITE
================================================

Pages:
 index.html        Home
 about.html        About
 services.html     All 18 cleaning services, detailed and searchable
 gallery.html      Illustrative photo gallery with filter and lightbox
 reviews.html      Review information (no made-up customer reviews)
 contact.html      Contact / email-based enquiry form

How to use:
 1. Keep these HTML files and the assets folder together.
 2. Open index.html in your browser.
 3. Upload the entire folder to GitHub Pages, Netlify, or another static host.
 4. For GitHub Pages, place the files at the publishing root.

No frameworks, build tools, backend, database or paid services are required.

IMPORTANT:
 - Photographs of services and rooms are loaded at runtime from Pexels stock
   photo CDN (internet connection required); the original mockup assets are
   bundled locally as fallbacks.
 - Stock photos are illustrative, not documented photos of Glow Grace jobs.
 - No customer reviews are invented. Replace the Reviews page content with
   genuine, authorized reviews as they become available.
 - The contact form PREPARES an email in the visitor's email application;
   it does not transmit data without sending the prepared email.
