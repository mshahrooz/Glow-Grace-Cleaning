A LITTLE ABOUT US

We don’t just clean.
We care for your space.

Glow Grace Cleaning serves Victoria, British Columbia, with a range of services for homes, apartments and workspaces. Our goal is simple: to help you spend less time worrying about cleaning and more time enjoying the place you’re in.

Whether you’re booking a regular refresh, getting ready for a move or giving your space extra attention, we aim to make cleaning feel straightforward, flexible and personal.

We work on an hourly basis and also take full or short-term contracts, so you can choose an approach that fits your needs.
